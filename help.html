Help & Support

Common questions about using Kabadiwala Connect. Can't find what you need? Reach out on our Contact page.

How do I request a pickup?

From the homepage, pick a kabadiwala near you, add the items you're selling to your cart, then tap "Request Pickup" and fill in your details.

What if my item isn't in the list?

Use the "Others" box below the item list — describe what you're selling and an estimated weight, and the kabadiwala will quote a rate in person.

How will the kabadiwala know about my request?

Once you submit your details, they're sent directly to our team, and we connect you with the kabadiwala you selected.

Are the prices fixed?

The listed rates are current market estimates. Final pricing is confirmed by the kabadiwala at the time of pickup, based on quantity and condition.
