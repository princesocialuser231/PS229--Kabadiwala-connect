Our Story

Kabadiwala Connect started with a simple observation: the people who quietly keep our cities clean — the kabadiwalas who collect and sort recyclable waste every day — are almost invisible to the formal systems built around them.

The recycling chain has a missing link.

India generates an enormous amount of recyclable waste every day. Yet, behind the formal recycling industry lies a vast network of informal waste collectors and kabadiwalas who play a crucial role in collecting and recovering valuable materials. They are an essential part of the recycling chain—but they often remain outside the formal system and work in dark . Scrapdeal was born from one simple question: What if technology could bring these unsung contributors into the formal recycling ecosystem?. While exploring the Smart India Hackathon problem statements under Clean & Green Technology, we came across the problem statement "Kabadiwala Connect - Bringing the Informal Collector into the Formal Recycling Chain".

The Problem

Millions of households rely on informal waste collectors, but there's no easy way to schedule a pickup, no transparency in pricing, and no record of the transaction afterward. For the kabadiwala, this means no proof of income, no credit history, and no way to access loans or government schemes meant for informal workers. Govt is also trying to address these problems and they launched several programs to help them like MUDRA for loans.

Our Idea

We built a platform where households can browse nearby kabadiwalas, see fair, transparent prices for what they're selling, and request a pickup in a few taps. Every request we receive is passed directly to the right kabadiwala, so the connection actually happens — not just in an app, but in real life.

How We're Different

Most scrap platforms list a single price per material — plastic, metal, e-waste, rubber — as if scrap arrives that neatly sorted. In Delhi, it almost never does. Kabadiwala Connect is built around how scrap actually shows up at the door: The method we bring will help users to sell their scrap to a trusted scrapdealer without having though of getting scammed

1

We deal in mixed items, not just clean categories

A cooler fan has copper winding inside, a plastic blade, and a metal body — three materials in one object. Beer bottles are glass, an old cycle is rubber tyres on a metal frame. Rather than forcing these into one price bucket, we let households list the actual item, and the kabadiwala breaks it down and prices it fairly, piece by piece.

2

We're honest about weighing

Households don't own weighing scales, so there's no way to know an item's exact weight in advance. Only the kabadiwala, who brings their own weighing equipment (small or large, depending on the load), can confirm it on the spot. Our listed rates are estimates to plan around — the final weight and price are always confirmed at pickup, not promised upfront.

3

Transparency backed by our team

If something feels off during a pickup, the customer isn't left to sort it out alone with the dealer — they can reach our team directly, since every request already passes through us before it reaches the kabadiwala.

Over time, we want every transaction on this platform to build a digital record for the kabadiwala — one that can eventually help them access fair credit, recognition, and inclusion in the formal economy they've always been a part of, just never counted in.
