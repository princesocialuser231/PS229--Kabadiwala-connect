Our Story

Kabadiwala Connect started with a simple observation: the people who quietly keep our cities clean — the kabadiwalas who collect and sort recyclable waste every day — are almost invisible to the formal systems built around them.

The Problem

Millions of households rely on informal waste collectors, but there's no easy way to schedule a pickup, no transparency in pricing, and no record of the transaction afterward. For the kabadiwala, this means no proof of income, no credit history, and no way to access loans or government schemes meant for informal workers.

Our Idea

We built a platform where households can browse nearby kabadiwalas, see fair, transparent prices for what they're selling, and request a pickup in a few taps. Every request we receive is passed directly to the right kabadiwala, so the connection actually happens — not just in an app, but in real life.

Our Vision

Over time, we want every transaction on this platform to build a digital record for the kabadiwala — one that can eventually help them access fair credit, recognition, and inclusion in the formal economy they've always been a part of, just never counted in.
